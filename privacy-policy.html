Privacy Policy

Aurum Empire  ·  Yan Labs  ·  Last updated: April 7, 2026

We built Aurum Empire with privacy in mind. We collect only what is strictly necessary to run the game and process purchases. We do not sell your data, we do not run ads, and we do not track your behavior for marketing purposes.

1. Who We Are

Aurum Empire is developed and operated by Yan Labs. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at ianpylypiuk@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Aurum Empire:

  • Account data — your email address and chosen username, provided when you register.
  • Game data — your in-game progress, balance, inventory, production facilities, shipments, and other gameplay state stored in our database.
  • Purchase data — transaction identifiers and subscription status processed by RevenueCat and Apple App Store. We do not store your payment card details.
  • Device identifier — an anonymous identifier used by RevenueCat to link purchases to your account across devices. This identifier is not tied to your name or email.
  • Support messages — if you contact us via the in-app support form, we store your email, subject, and message.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or any other sensitive device data. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

3. Push Notifications

In the future, Aurum Empire may offer push notifications (for example, to alert you when a shipment arrives or a game event occurs). If we add this feature, we will ask for your permission first. Your push notification token will be stored solely for the purpose of delivering notifications to your device and will not be shared with third parties.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your game account.
  • To save and synchronize your game progress across sessions and devices.
  • To process in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the game.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the game.

5. Third-Party Services

Aurum Empire uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider. Your account data and game state are stored on Supabase servers. Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — processes and validates in-app purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store — handles payment processing for purchases made on iOS. Apple Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and game data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data will be permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days. Support tickets are retained for up to 2 years for quality assurance purposes.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable privacy laws (including GDPR and CCPA), such as:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to correct inaccurate data.
  • The right to request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • The right to data portability.
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at ianpylypiuk@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Aurum Empire is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly. If you believe your child has provided us with their data, please contact us.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and secure authentication. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. For significant changes, we will notify you within the app.
