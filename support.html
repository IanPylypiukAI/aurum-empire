Frequently Asked Questions

My purchase isn't being applied — what should I do?
First, make sure you have a stable internet connection and restart the app. If the issue persists, tap "Restore Purchases" in the in-app Shop. If it still doesn't work, contact us with your Apple ID email and we'll resolve it manually.
How do I restore purchases on a new device?
Log in with the same Apple ID you used to make the original purchase, then tap "Restore Purchases" in the Shop. Your VIP status will be restored automatically.
The app crashes or won't load — how do I fix it?
Try force-closing and reopening the app. If the problem continues, delete and reinstall it — your progress is saved server-side and won't be lost. Make sure your iOS version is up to date.
How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?
We love hearing from players. Send us an email with a description of the issue or idea. Screenshots are always helpful. We read every message.
